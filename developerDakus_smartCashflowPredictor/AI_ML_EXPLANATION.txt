WHAT DOES THIS PROJECT DO?
---------------------------
It takes a business's monthly financial data (uploaded as a CSV file)
and uses AI/ML techniques to:
  - Predict if the business is at risk of running out of cash
  - Score the overall financial health (0 to 100)
  - Generate smart insights and recommendations automatically
  - Export a full PDF financial report


================================================================
1. LINEAR REGRESSION — Trend Detection (Python: model.py)
================================================================

WHAT IT IS:
  Linear Regression is one of the most fundamental machine learning
  algorithms. It finds the "slope" or direction of a series of numbers
  over time — basically, is this going UP, DOWN, or FLAT?

HOW WE USED IT:
  We wrote a custom linear regression function (linear_slope) in Python
  that works on monthly financial data WITHOUT any external ML library.
  It calculates the exact mathematical slope using the standard formula.

WHAT WE DETECTED:
  - Revenue trend: Is monthly revenue going up or down over time?
  - Cost trend: Are expenses rising faster than revenue? (Margin compression)
  - Profit trend: Is the business becoming more or less profitable month by month?

REAL EXAMPLE:
  If revenue was [10000, 10500, 11200, 10800, 10300], the slope is
  slightly negative — the model catches this and raises an alert saying
  "Revenue shows a declining trend of X% per month."

WHY IT MATTERS:
  Simple averages can't tell you direction. Linear regression gives you
  the actual trend, which is critical for predicting the future.

2. RULE-BASED AI / EXPERT SYSTEM (advisorService.js + model.py)
================================================================

WHAT IT IS:
  A Rule-Based AI (also called an Expert System) encodes human financial
  expertise into IF-THEN rules. It mimics how a professional financial
  advisor would look at the numbers and give advice.

HOW WE USED IT:
  We built a full rule engine in both Python (primary) and JavaScript
  (fallback). It evaluates 6 financial dimensions:

  a) RUNWAY RULES
     - If runway < 3 months → "CRITICAL: Only X months of cash left"
     - If runway > 18 months → "Healthy runway, consider investing surplus"

  b) EXPENSE RATIO RULES
     - If expenses > 130% of revenue → "Critically over-spending"
     - If expenses are 75% of revenue → "Great cost discipline"

  c) REVENUE GROWTH RULES
     - If average monthly growth < -5% → "Revenue declining, intervention needed"
     - If average monthly growth > +10% → "Strong growth, scale your channels"

  d) LOSS STREAK RULES
     - If 3+ consecutive months of losses → "Turnaround plan needed immediately"
     - If 1 loss month → "Monitor closely, could be seasonal"

  e) LOAN BURDEN RULES
     - If loan EMIs > 25% of revenue → "Heavy debt burden, consider refinancing"

  f) INVENTORY RULES
     - If inventory > 35% of total costs → "Possibly overstocked"

WHY IT MATTERS:
  Rules are explainable — you can always say WHY a recommendation was made.
  This is the core intelligence behind the AI Advisor panel in the dashboard.

3. WEIGHTED SCORING MODEL — Business Health Score (healthScoreService.js)
================================================================

WHAT IT IS:
  A Weighted Scoring Model is a simple but powerful technique used in
  credit scoring, risk assessment, and financial ratings. Different
  factors are assigned different importance weights and combined into
  one final score (0 to 100).

HOW WE USED IT:
  We compute 5 individual component scores (each from 0 to 100):

  Component              | Weight | What it measures
  -----------------------|--------|------------------------------------------
  Runway Score           | 28%    | How many months of cash is left
  Burn Rate Score        | 22%    | How many months had negative cashflow
  Revenue Growth Score   | 20%    | Month-over-month revenue growth trend
  Expense Ratio Score    | 18%    | How efficiently revenue covers costs
  Consecutive Loss Score | 12%    | Penalises streaks of monthly losses

  Final Score = (RunwayScore × 0.28) + (BurnRate × 0.22) + ... etc.

SCORE INTERPRETATION:
  80-100 → Excellent (healthy and growing)
  65-79  → Good (stable with minor concerns)
  45-64  → Stable (watch carefully)
  25-44  → Poor (needs attention)
  0-24   → Critical (immediate action required)

WHY IT MATTERS:
  Instead of looking at 10 different numbers, the business owner gets
  ONE clear score — just like a credit score — that summarizes everything.

4. ADDITIVE RISK SCORING — Risk Classification (model.py + insightsEngine.js)
================================================================

WHAT IT IS:
  Additive Risk Scoring is the core logic behind many fraud detection
  and risk classification systems. Each bad signal adds points to a
  risk counter, and the total determines the risk level.

HOW WE USED IT:
  Each negative financial signal adds risk points:

  Signal                          | Risk Points Added
  --------------------------------|-------------------
  Revenue declining (slope < 0)   | +2
  Profit margins compressing      | +1
  Expense ratio >= 130%           | +2
  Expense ratio >= 100%           | +1
  Negative cash position          | +3  (critical!)
  Cash < 2 months of expenses     | +1
  Loan burden > 20% of revenue    | +1
  >50% of months were loss-making | +1

  FINAL CLASSIFICATION:
    Total >= 5 → HIGH RISK
    Total >= 2 → MEDIUM RISK
    Total < 2  → LOW RISK

WHY IT MATTERS:
  This is the same logic used by banks and insurance companies to
  classify risk. It's simple, transparent, and effective.

5. PYTHON + JAVASCRIPT HYBRID ARCHITECTURE (insightsEngine.js)
================================================================

WHAT IT IS:
  The system tries to use a Python ML model first. If Python is not
  available on the machine, it automatically falls back to a pure
  JavaScript version of the same logic.

HOW IT WORKS:
  1. Server receives the financial data
  2. It tries to spawn a Python process and run model.py
  3. If Python succeeds → use Python's linear regression + analysis
  4. If Python fails (not installed) → JS fallback engine kicks in
  5. Either way, the user gets the same quality of insights

WHY IT MATTERS:
  This makes the project portable and reliable. It works on any machine
  regardless of whether Python is installed — a real production concern
  that most hackathon projects ignore.

6. CASHFLOW RUNWAY PREDICTION (calculationService.js)
================================================================

WHAT IT IS:
  A financial forecasting technique to predict HOW LONG a business
  can survive before running out of money.

FORMULA USED:
  Runway (months) = Latest Closing Cash / Average Monthly Loss

HOW WE CALCULATED IT:
  - Parse every month's data: Revenue, Fixed Cost, Variable Cost,
    Inventory Cost, Loan EMI
  - Calculate Net Profit = Revenue - Total Costs
  - Calculate Closing Cash = Opening Cash + Net Profit
  - Count how many months had negative profit (burn months)
  - Average those losses → Average Monthly Burn Rate
  - Divide current cash by burn rate → Runway in months

REAL-TIME RISK FLAGS:
  The system also flags risks IN REAL TIME while reading the CSV:
  - If closing cash goes negative → "CRITICAL: Negative cash in [Month]"
  - If 2+ consecutive loss months → "HIGH RISK: Consecutive losses by [Month]"
  - If revenue drops for 2+ months → "DECLINING REVENUE detected"

WHY IT MATTERS:
  Runway prediction is literally what separates a surviving startup from
  a dead one. Many businesses fail because they don't see the cash crisis
  coming. This predicts it months in advance.
